Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to Sai Renuka Cab. This policy explains how we handle customer information.

Introduction

Sai Renuka Cab respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information shared by customers while using our website or booking our cab services.

Information We Collect

When customers contact us or book a cab, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Pickup and drop locations
  • Travel details required for booking

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used for:

  • Confirming cab bookings
  • Providing travel updates
  • Contacting customers regarding services
  • Improving customer experience
  • Managing customer enquiries

Data Protection

Sai Renuka Cab takes reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or share customer personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information may only be shared when required to provide the requested cab service.

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. Sai Renuka Cab is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Customer Rights

Customers can contact us to update, correct or request information regarding their personal details shared with Sai Renuka Cab.

Policy Updates
