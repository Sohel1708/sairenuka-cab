ABOUT THE DESTINATION

Comfortable Mumbai To Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Journey

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, formerly known as Aurangabad, is one of Maharashtra's most important historical and industrial cities. The city is also a major hub for manufacturing, education, healthcare and business.

HERITAGE & TOURISM

Explore World Famous Attractions

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar is one of India's leading heritage destinations, attracting visitors from across the world.

Ajanta Caves

Discover the UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its ancient Buddhist rock-cut caves and magnificent paintings.

Ellora Caves

Visit the famous Kailasa Temple and explore one of India's greatest architectural masterpieces.

Bibi Ka Maqbara

Experience the iconic Mughal monument often referred to as the "Mini Taj Mahal of the Deccan."

Daulatabad Fort

Explore the historic hill fort renowned for its impressive military architecture and panoramic views.

Grishneshwar Temple

Visit one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas dedicated to Lord Shiva near Ellora.

Paithan

Plan a comfortable trip to the historic town famous for Paithani sarees and the Jayakwadi Dam.
